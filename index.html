Our Eye Care Services

From routine eye examinations to prescription spectacles, contact lenses and eye health screenings - professional eye care tailored to your visual needs.

Comprehensive Eye Examination

A thorough assessments of your vision and eye health, including binocular vision, color vision, and early signs of eye conditions - helping you see clearly and comfortably.

Prescription Spectacles

Spectacles tailored to your lifestyle, with lens options including single vision, bifocal, progressive, and specialised lenses.

Contact Lens Fittings

Professional fitting for all visual needs - from first-time wearers to astigmatism, presbyopia, and specialised requirements - with assessment, guidance, and follow-up care.

Paediatric Vision Care

Child-friendly eye assessments to evaluate vision and visual development, including binocular vision assessment and myopia management for healthy vision growth.

Cataract Screening

Cataract screening to assess for signs of cataracts and vision changes, with a clear explanation of findings and appropriate advise on next steps.

Retinal Screening

Advanced retinal imaging to screen for diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, and glaucoma - often before symptoms appear.

From Alvision Optical to Alvision Optometrist

Serving the Cheras community since 2003, Alvision began as a small neighbourhood optical shop. In 2024, we transformed into Alvision Optometrist, expanding our practice with advanced diagnostic instruments for comprehensive eye examinations and eye health assessments. Today, we continue to provide personalised eye care while staying true to our neighbourhood roots.
